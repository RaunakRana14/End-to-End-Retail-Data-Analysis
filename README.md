End-to-End Retail Data Analysis

## Overview

This project is to analyzes the Superstore Retail dataset. The main and focused objective of this analysis on Superstore 
dataset is to transform raw business  data into meaningful insights by performing data cleaning, data analysis and visualization.
Using Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI, this project identifies important trends, profit-loss patterns, and areas where 
business performance can be improved, helping support better data-driven decisions.

## Problem Statement
 
This project aims to analyze retail sales data to:

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability performance.
- Identify the products and categories that contribute most to sales and profit.
- Determine whether high-sales products are also highly profitable.
- Identify products or categories with low or negative profitability.
- Analyze sales and profit performance across different regions and customer segments.
- Understand sales and profitability trends over time.
- Examine the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Identify underperforming areas and potential opportunities for improvement.
- Convert the analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.
