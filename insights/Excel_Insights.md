1. By Statistical Summary
   - Sales and profit show high variability, with a small number of high-value transactions influencing the overall results.        Profitability is uneven, as some transactions generate negative profit and margins, while discount levels vary across          transactions. Overall, the data indicates significant differences in sales and profitability across transactions.

2. Category Performance
   - Technology has the highest sales and profit, with a 17% profit margin.
   -  Office Supplies has the highest quantity sold and also maintains a 17% profit margin.
   -  Furniture has strong sales but only a 2% profit margin, showing comparatively low profitability.
  
   #Business Implications

      - Review Furniture pricing, costs, and discounts to improve its low profit margin.

6. Customer Segment Analysis
   - Consumer is the strongest segment in terms of sales, profit, and quantity, while Home Office contributes the least           across all three metrics. Corporate performs between the two segments with a strong overall contribution.

7. Time Trend Analysis
   -Sales and profit show a clear upward trend from 2014 to 2017, with 2017 recording the highest sales and profit. This         indicates overall business performance improved over the four-year period.

8. Discount Analysis
   - 0% discount generates the highest sales and profit, with a 30% profit margin.
   - 10–20% discounts remain profitable, but margins decline from 17% to 12%.
   - Discounts of 30% and above result in negative profit margins.
   - Higher discounts lead to increasing losses, with 80% discount resulting in a -180% margin.

  #Business Implications
   - Keep discounts at profitable levels, particularly below 30%, and reduce excessive discounting at 30% and above to avoid      losses.

9. Sales Growth
    - Sales increased substantially over the period, with growth accelerating after 2015.

  #Business Implications
    - Focus on factors that supported growth after 2015.
    - Monitor periods of decline to identify and address causes early.

10. Monthly Trend Analysis
    -  November has the highest sales and quantity, while December has the highest profit.
    - September–December are the strongest-performing months overall.
    - February has the lowest sales and quantity, while January has the lowest profit.

  #Business Implications
    -Increase inventory and sales efforts during September–December.
    -Use targeted promotions in January–February to improve low performance.
    -Ensure sufficient stock during November–December to meet high demand.


Final Conclusion

-Overall performance is strong and improving, with sales and profit showing an upward trend over the years.
-Technology and Office Supplies are the strongest categories, while Furniture has comparatively low profitability.
-High-margin products should be prioritized, while low-profit and loss-making products need improvement.
-Excessive discounting reduces profitability, with discounts of 30% and above resulting in negative margins.
-West is the strongest region, while Central needs greater focus on profitability.
-Consumer is the strongest customer segment, while Home Office contributes the least.
-September–December are the strongest months, requiring better inventory and sales planning.
