# Python Analysis Insights


Python & Advanced Analysis Findings----


       -Technology generated the highest overall profit, while Furniture showed weaker profitability.
   
       -West had the highest sales and profit, while Central had the lowest profit margin.
   
      -Several high-sales products generated negative profit, showing that high sales do not always mean high profitability.
   
      -Higher discounts were strongly associated with lower profit; discount above 20% showed negative overall profitability.
   
      -RFM analysis identified Regular customers as the largest segment, while At Risk customers represented a significant           high revenue group.
   
     -ANOVA confirmed a statistically significant difference in Profit across categories.


Main ML Findings----


      -Linear Regression was the best overall model with 72.02% R² and 116.47 RMSE.
   
      -Discount was the strongest predictor of Profit, with a coefficient of -155.79.
   
      -ML results reinforced the finding that higher discounting negatively affects profitability.
   
      -The model provides useful overall profit estimates but has noticeable errors for individual transactions.

Business Recommendations----


      -Control excessive discounts, especially above 20–30%.
   
      -Review high-sales but loss-making products and their pricing strategies.
   
      -Focus on high-profit products and profitable categories.
   
      -Investigate low profitability in Furniture and Central region.
   
      -Prioritize At Risk and High Value customers with targeted retention strategies.
   
      -Use the ML model as a supporting tool for profitability and pricing decisions.
